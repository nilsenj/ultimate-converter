Privacy notice

Ultimate Converter for Windows · Updated 13 October 2026

Your files

The app reads files you choose and processes conversions locally. File contents can include personal information. Conversion does not upload them to the developer or a conversion server. Originals remain unchanged; you choose where to save new outputs.

Local history and recovery

The app stores saved-file history and conversion recovery information on your device. Temporary working files and unsaved results may remain while a session is active or recoverable. Use the app’s history and recovery controls to remove records or discard recoverable work. Clearing history does not delete your saved output files.

Accounts and analytics

No account is required to use conversion features. This Windows release does not include developer analytics or advertising tracking in the app.

External links and support

Opening the website, GitHub, Patreon or Base takes you to an external service with its own privacy practices. Optional Base support purchases are handled by Base/monobank. The converter does not receive your payment-card details. All conversion features remain free.

Reports you choose to send

If you submit a GitHub issue or contact the developer, the information you provide is shared through that service. GitHub issues are public: do not attach private documents, credentials or personal information. Use a non-sensitive example where possible.

Website and Store

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and contains no added analytics script. Hosting providers and Microsoft Store may process connection, account and diagnostic information under their own notices.

Contact and changes

Developer: Ivan Nikolenko. For questions, use the project issue tracker without posting sensitive information. This notice will be updated when relevant app behavior changes.
