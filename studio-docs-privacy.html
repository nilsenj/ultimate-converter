Studio Docs Privacy Policy

Studio Docs is published by Ivan Nikolenko at Studio N. For privacy questions, email hello@studio-n.work.

Documents and signatures

Studio Docs opens, signs, and edits PDFs on your device. Recent file references, editing drafts, preferences, and saved signatures stay on the device. The app does not collect or share user data. When you export a PDF, you choose the destination. If you choose a cloud storage provider from the system file picker, that provider handles the copy under its own privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Local drafts and saved signatures stay on the device until removed. Clearing recent files also deletes local editing drafts. Password values entered in PDF form fields are excluded from drafts. On Android, app data is excluded from backups and device transfer. Clearing app storage removes drafts, preferences, and saved signatures. Exported copies are managed separately at the chosen destination.

Accounts, ads, and purchases

Studio Docs has no account registration or advertising. Google Play handles purchase and installation information under Google's Privacy Policy.
