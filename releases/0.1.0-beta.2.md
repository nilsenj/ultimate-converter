## New in Beta 2

- **Keep HDR:** convert supported PQ/HLG video to 10-bit VP9 WebM, retaining supported HDR color and static mastering/light-level metadata.
- **HDR to SDR:** convert supported HDR video to ordinary-screen H.264 MP4 or VP9 WebM with tone mapping and BT.709 output.
- **Resize and shrink:** choose original resolution, 1080p or 720p, quality presets or an output-size goal. The popup explains color changes and metadata losses before conversion.
- **Supported Dolby Vision:** profile 8 with HDR10-compatible base layer (compatibility 1, no enhancement layer). Re-encoding uses that HDR10 base and requires consent to discard dynamic Dolby Vision/HDR10+ metadata. Copying supported HEVC tracks into MKV preserves their verified metadata without shrinking.
- Windows media inputs up to **50 GB**, video up to **4K / 60 fps**, storage preflight/reserve checks, conversion progress and Cancel. Duration has no fixed cap.
- Keep the same rounded translucent format popup, safe Save, **Open file** links and persistent history of the last **20** successful saves.

Also included: still-image formats, audio conversion/extraction, selected camera RAW and declared raw-video imports, TXT/Markdown/HTML/RTF/DOCX/ODT/PDF conversions, LOG export and structured JSON/XML conversion, formatting and minification.

## Limits worth knowing

- **Unsigned public beta.** Windows may show an unknown-publisher/reputation warning. Clean-machine, broader hardware and accessibility checks remain pending. Only Windows x64 is packaged here.
- HDR playback needs a compatible player/display. Dolby Vision profile 5 and enhancement-layer workflows are unsupported. HEVC Main10 encoding is unavailable. HDR MP4 stream-copy remains gated; use MKV for supported unchanged HEVC copying.
- Audio selection currently supports compatible mono/stereo tracks. No automatic surround downmix. Choose a supported stereo track if the source also includes surround tracks.
- A size goal is an upper limit, not a guaranteed result. Jobs unable to meet it fail safely without saving an invalid output. Quality presets can produce larger files. Disk estimates cannot guarantee space on every destination; save failures retain the validated result for retry.
- Full-length 50 GB movies, sustained 4K thermal performance, HDR-screen visual fidelity and broad mobile-player compatibility have not been established by this release's bounded tests.
- Complex document layouts can reflow; PDF-to-editable conversion reconstructs text. No OCR for scanned PDFs. Still images are limited to 32 MiB and 4 million pixels; some RAW/codec variants are unsupported. Archive conversion is not included.
